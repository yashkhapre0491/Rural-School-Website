Features & Services⚙️🌱

Simple, strong and locally-focused — what we do to make education real and useful for village children.

📚 Learning Support

Small groups, remedial classes, and daily reading to build strong basics in reading & maths.

Tip: 20 minutes reading every day boosts confidence greatly!

👩‍🏫 Teacher Training

Monthly workshops for teachers with easy-to-make teaching aids and local examples.

We teach with stories, games and the school garden.

🌿 School Garden

Hands-on science — kids plant seeds, observe growth and learn nutrition and nature care.

SDG link: Practical skills + environment awareness.

💻 Basic Digital Skills

Shared tablet sessions and simple offline videos to introduce computers and typing.

Skill for future jobs — starting small, thinking big.

Quick Tips & Tools 🛠️

  • Flashcards from cardboard — low-cost and effective ✅
  • Reading buddies: older students help younger ones 📖
  • Use local stories to teach language & values 🗣️
  • Short quizzes + prizes to encourage participation 🏅

Programs & Community 🌟

We believe education is a community effort. Parents, teachers and village elders meet monthly to plan events, support students, and share skills. The school runs sports day, health camps and festivals — all linked to learning.

Our Promise — Short Lines that Mean a Lot ✨

Gramjyoti Rural Public School — “Lighting the village with knowledge.”

  • We teach to make lives better, not just exams. 🎯
  • We build skills for work, life and responsible citizenship. 🤝
  • We include every child — especially girls and first-generation learners. 👩‍🎓
